Gletir Tracker

Chrome extension that reads your account statement on the Gletir platform when you sign in and builds your history: balance, daily evolution and annualized return (XIRR), plus volatility, drawdown, diversification, rebalancing and projection. Free, with a one-time PRO upgrade of US$ 9 (license via Gumroad).

Independent project, unofficial and not affiliated with Gletir. Screenshots use sample data.

  • Highlights
  • Captures your account statement when you sign in to Gletir and builds your history day by day.
  • Free: total balance, charts by asset type and export to PDF, CSV and JSON.
  • PRO: annualized return (XIRR), risk analysis, diversification, rebalancing, projection and Google Sheets sync.
  • Your data is stored only in your browser: no server of my own and no trackers.
  • One-time PRO license through Gumroad, valid for up to 3 computers.

Easy PDF Free

Ad-free PDF reader with no accounts, made to open a file with one tap from WhatsApp. Works as a web app (PDF.js) and as an Android app with Capacitor.

Tool

Kiosko Digital

A viewer of newspaper front pages from several countries with its own back end that collects the images, converts them to WebP and updates itself, with an admin panel and offline mode.

The code is not public. The front pages belong to their newspapers and are shown only as a technical reference.

  • Highlights
  • PHP scraper with about 27 sources from 15 countries: each outlet is declared with its URL and CSS selector, and the bot identifies itself and waits between requests.
  • Downloads the front pages, builds WebP thumbnails and registers each outlet automatically.
  • Viewer with a country filter and progressive loading, installable as a PWA with offline mode.
  • Password-protected admin panel: database status, a per-source report of the last run and a button to request an update.
  • Cron jobs, rotated logs and secrets kept outside the web folder.
  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • Web scraping
  • PWA

Moova Tracker

Chrome extension (Manifest V3) that watches shipment status and alerts you by notification, email or push when it changes. No backend. Unofficial project.

  • Highlights
  • Checks one or more shipments every 5 to 60 minutes from a service worker.
  • Alerts with a Chrome notification and a colored badge when the status changes.
  • Shows an arrival estimate and can alert you when the courier is close.
  • Optional channels: email (EmailJS) and push (ntfy) work well; WhatsApp (CallMeBot) is unreliable.
  • No backend: settings live in chrome.storage.local. Unofficial project, not affiliated with Moova.
  • Chrome MV3
  • Service worker
  • ntfy
  • EmailJS
