My story

From graphic design to code

I started in 2012 as a web designer. I never worked as one, but that is where I come from: from design I moved into marketing, where I used Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Search Console every day. I learned to look at what people do on a page and what makes them come back.

Since 2014 I have been building websites, and over the years I went from implementing them to programming them end to end. I studied Analista Programador at ORT, worked as a Web Developer at Infragistics Uruguay and, at the same time, kept working on my own projects and on clients such as AUF, Urugol and BROU.

What I enjoy most is front-end: the point where design, performance and code meet. That is why my sites are measured with Lighthouse, and everything you see on this page is published and can be tried.

When a tool doesn't exist, I build it: Gletir Tracker (an extension published on the Chrome Web Store), Easy PDF Free or Kiosko Digital. I use AI to speed up the repetitive parts, but I review and test everything myself before delivering.
